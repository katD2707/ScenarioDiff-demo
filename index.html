01 / THE PROBLEM

A forecast can miss the event hiding in plain sight.

A traffic series may look steady while a road closure is announced. Medicine sales may look routine while a local outbreak is taking shape. These clues live in documents, news, and logs, often scattered across time.

ScenarioDiff gives each clue a job: ground the past, describe a possible future, or guide a short part of the forecast.

02 / HOW IT WORKS

From evidence
to a future you can inspect.

Three focused agents prepare explicit guidance. A diffusion model generates trajectories; anchor blending then refines only the event-relevant regions.

03⌖

Place sparse anchors

The Anchor Guidance Agent proposes plausible value intervals at a few event-relevant future steps.

04↝

Shape the forecast

The Multimodal Diffusion Transformer generates possible paths. Anchor Blended Sampling adjusts local regions without retraining.

Many plausible futures

All agent outputs use only information available before the forecast starts. The paper evaluates the method on Time-MMD; the applications below are illustrative.
