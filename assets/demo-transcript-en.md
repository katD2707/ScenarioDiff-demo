# ScenarioDiff — English presentation

History and ground truth are published observations. Event reports are sourced. Forecasts and anchor intervals are illustrative retrospective examples, not ScenarioDiff model predictions or a point-in-time evaluation.

## 00:00:00.000 — intro
An event can change the future before that change appears in the historical series.

## 00:00:09.000 — method
Historical evidence informs a scenario. Sparse anchors identify local regions of interest; diffusion generates the trajectory.
